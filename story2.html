The Unseen Force

“The Unseen Force” tells the mysterious story of an invisible energy that shapes the destiny of those who encounter it. Set in a small village, strange phenomena begin to unfold — objects move on their own, whispers echo in empty rooms, and people start questioning reality itself.

A young physicist, Sarah, decides to uncover the truth behind these mysterious powers. What she discovers challenges everything we know about science, life, and the forces that bind the universe.
