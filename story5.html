The Space Visitor

One quiet night, a young boy named Leo was awakened by a strange glow outside his window.
Hovering in his backyard was a small, silver spacecraft — and from it emerged a friendly alien visitor. Together, they explored the wonders of the universe, learning that friendship knows no boundaries — not even those between worlds.

"The Space Visitor" is a heartwarming tale of curiosity, courage, and the beauty of discovering the unknown.
